\# Generative AI



\## What is Generative AI?



Generative AI is a branch of Artificial Intelligence capable of creating new content such as text, code, images, audio, and videos.



\---



\## Traditional AI vs Generative AI



Traditional AI



\- Classification

\- Prediction

\- Recognition



Generative AI



\- Creates new content

\- Generates code

\- Produces text and images



\---



\## Applications



\- Chatbots

\- Code Generation

\- Content Creation

\- Image Generation

\- Documentation

\- Software Development
