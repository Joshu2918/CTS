\# Prompt Engineering



\## What is Prompt Engineering?



Prompt Engineering is the process of designing clear and effective prompts to obtain accurate responses from AI models.



\---



\## Prompting Techniques



\### Zero-shot Prompting



Provide only the task.



\### Few-shot Prompting



Provide examples before the task.



\### Chain-of-Thought Prompting



Guide the model through reasoning step by step.



\---



\## Best Practices



\- Be specific

\- Provide context

\- Specify output format

\- Break complex tasks into steps

\- Refine prompts iteratively



\---



\## Ethical Considerations



\- Avoid bias

\- Protect privacy

\- Verify AI responses

\- Use AI responsibly
