\# Agile Concepts



\## What is Agile?



Agile is an iterative software development methodology that focuses on delivering working software quickly through collaboration and continuous feedback.



\---



\## Agile Manifesto Values



1\. Individuals and interactions over processes and tools



2\. Working software over comprehensive documentation



3\. Customer collaboration over contract negotiation



4\. Responding to change over following a plan



\---



\## Agile Principles



\- Customer satisfaction

\- Frequent software delivery

\- Welcome changing requirements

\- Daily collaboration

\- Sustainable development

\- Continuous improvement



\---



\## Agile vs Waterfall



\### Agile



\- Iterative

\- Flexible

\- Continuous delivery

\- Customer feedback



\### Waterfall



\- Sequential

\- Fixed scope

\- Late testing

\- Limited feedback



\---



\## Benefits of Agile



\- Faster delivery

\- Better quality

\- Increased customer satisfaction

\- Improved collaboration

\- Lower project risk
